Pie Chart of Life

Every night of your life, counted by where you slept. Sketch it in a few minutes, then make it more exact whenever you like.

This is an example life. Everything below is made up, so you can see how it works.

1You

Your name is only used on your pie. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

2Places you've lived

Start where you were born, then add each move. Name places the way you'd say them (a country, a state, or a city) and use the same name every time, so the slices add up.

3Time away

Trips count by nights: a Friday-to-Sunday weekend is 2 nights. Start with the big ones, like a semester abroad or a long summer somewhere.

Things you did most years Summers at the beach, holidays with family

4Bring in your phone's history optional

If Google Maps Timeline has been on, your phone already knows where you slept for years. Your file is read right here on your device and never uploaded. Where it has data, it replaces what you typed.

  1. Open the Google Maps app.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right), then Your timeline.
  3. Tap ⋯ (top right), then Location and privacy settings.
  4. Tap Export Timeline data and save the file to Files.
  5. Come back here and choose that file below.
  1. Open your phone's Settings, then Location.
  2. Tap Location services, then Timeline.
  3. Tap Export Timeline data, then Continue and Save.
  4. Come back here and choose the file (it's in Downloads).
Older Google Takeout exports (Records.json or the monthly Semantic Location History files) work too.
